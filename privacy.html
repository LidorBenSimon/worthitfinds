Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Worth It Finds ("we") shares hand-picked product deals on this website, on Telegram, Instagram and Pinterest. This page explains what data we handle.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, ads trackers or forms, and we do not collect any personal information from visitors. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Affiliate links

Deal links are affiliate links to AliExpress. When you click one, you leave this site and AliExpress's own privacy policy and cookies apply. We may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We receive only anonymous order totals and commission reports, never your name, address or payment details.

Pinterest, Telegram and Instagram

We use the official Pinterest API only to publish Pins to our own Pinterest account and boards, and the Telegram Bot API only to publish to our own channel. We do not read, collect or store data about other users of these platforms. Access tokens for our own accounts are stored privately and are never shared or sold.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13.
